Record the current web-search multi-snapshot gem5 serial run point using:
- `fw` from `init_warmed` to generate `snapshot_0` and `snapshot_1`
- no `.gem` emission during warming
- gem5 `run_sample` over `snapshot_0..snapshot_1`
- lazy per-snapshot conversion inside `run_sample`
- cleanup of derived gem5 conversion artifacts after each successful snapshot run

This package is intentionally narrow. It captures the final two-snapshot serial
workflow and the resulting outputs. It does not restate the earlier restore,
topology, or single-snapshot debugging history.
