Validate the multi-core TLB restore wiring after fixing the MMU source-file
contract mismatch.

This package is a 4-core smoke on `snapshot_0` that checks:

1. multi-core conversion generates `mmu-cpu0..3.cpt`
2. gem5 instantiates one `ArmTLBRestorer` per CPU
3. cold vs restored counters move in the expected direction on the same
   checkpoint and instruction bound

This is a wiring/functional-effect validation package, not a full multi-core
effectiveness study.
